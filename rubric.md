# Lab 07 - Capstone Iteration Studio Rubric

This is a studio completion rubric. It does not silently change the published course-component weights or non-compensatory assessment gates.

| Dimension | Points | Full-credit evidence |
|---|---:|---|
| V0 framing and scope | 10 | One-sentence player promise, explicit action/state/end/restart rules, screenshot, exclusions, and preserved tag. |
| V1 playable core | 15 | Another member can start, play one meaningful loop, reach an end condition, and restart. |
| Contracts and test design | 20 | At least four falsifiable contracts and six meaningful focused tests, including boundary, invariant, restart, and regression coverage. |
| V2 debug iteration | 15 | One genuine observed failure, smallest supported hypothesis and repair, focused rerun, full regression, and truthful evidence. |
| V3 playtest iteration | 15 | Human proxy observations, one justified change, acceptance evidence, and an honest unresolved limitation. |
| GitHub collaboration | 15 | Every member authors and reviews work; small branches/PRs, reviewed diffs, useful commits, clean tags, and no credential leakage. |
| Individual understanding and report seed | 10 | Every member explains one state decision, one test, one repair/review decision, and their own contribution. |
| **Total** | **100** | |

## Non-compensatory completion gates

A high total cannot compensate for any of these missing gates:

1. no playable and restartable V1;
2. fewer than six meaningful tests or all tests skipped;
3. no genuine red-to-green debug record;
4. one or more members have no authored and reviewed contribution;
5. no reproducible run from a clean clone;
6. invented command, test, playtest, Git, or AI-use evidence; or
7. a member cannot explain the submitted change they claim.

## Scope and polish rule

Visual polish, many levels, audio, menus, or a large feature list may earn no additional credit until the core gates are complete. An intentionally small, testable and explainable game is stronger evidence than a larger fragile game.
